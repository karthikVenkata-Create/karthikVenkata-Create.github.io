Costco Valuation Model

This is the DCF from my Costco project, running in your browser. Change any assumption and everything below updates. It starts from my October 20, 2026 live run, when Costco was at $992.35. My call is SELL with a $493 target. Try to talk me out of it.

Assumptions

Growth and margin fade in a straight line from year 1 to the final year. Starting numbers are Costco's FY2026 actuals: $303.2B revenue, $20.2B cash, $6.2B debt, 443.3M shares, 24% tax rate.

What is the price assuming?

Each line solves for one input that makes the DCF equal the share price, keeping everything else as set above.

For reference: Costco's revenue has compounded about 8.7% a year since FY2004, and its FY2026 operating margin was 3.85%.

What does a buyer get?

This flips the question around: if you pay the share price and your forecast plays out, this is the yearly return you earn. Stocks normally need several points more than Treasuries to be worth the risk.

10,000 simulations

Each run draws growth (±4 pts), margin (−0.5 to +1 pt), discount rate (±1.5 pts) and long-term growth (2–4%) around your inputs.
